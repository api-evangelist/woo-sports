GKA Big Air Challenge 2025 2–9 January, 2025 | Cape Town, South Africa The GKA is excited to unveil a groundbreaking new Big Air kiteboarding team competition, the GKA Big Air Challenge 2025, in collaboration with WOO Sports’ popular WOO sensor. Teams of riders, which may be made up of professionals and amateurs, will battle in Cape Town, South Africa, for glory. The highest jumps of the top five riders during the week of 2 to 9 January, 2025, will be combined, with the biggest team total getting the win.
